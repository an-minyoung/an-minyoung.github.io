## Current appointment

**Postdoctoral Fellow**, Walter H. Shorenstein Asia-Pacific Research Center, Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, Stanford University · July 2025–present

My research examines gender, skilled migration, career trajectories, and institutional inequality. [Stanford profile](https://aparc.fsi.stanford.edu/people/minyoung)

## Methods and tools

Quantitative and computational social science; analysis of population and survey data; in-depth interviews. Programming and analysis in **SQL, R, Python, and Stata**.

Research interests include gender, migration, researchers’ geographic mobility, higher education, statistics and methods, and computational social science.

**Language:** Korean (native).
