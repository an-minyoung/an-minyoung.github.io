## Current appointment

**Postdoctoral Fellow**, Walter H. Shorenstein Asia-Pacific Research Center, Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, Stanford University · July 2025–present

My research examines gender, skilled migration, career trajectories, and institutional inequality. [Stanford profile](https://aparc.fsi.stanford.edu/people/minyoung)

## Education

- **PhD in Sociology**, University of Arizona · 2025<br>
  Dissertation: _Do Gender and Gender Inequality Inform Migration Decisions of Skilled Workers?_
- **Certificate in Computational Social Science**, University of Arizona · 2021
- **MA in Sociology**, Ewha Womans University · 2016
- **BA with Honors in Political Science**, Ewha Womans University · 2013<br>
  Double major in Society and Justice; minor in Law.

## Research experience

At the University of Arizona, I contributed to projects on international research careers, medical specialization, art activism, and political identity. Before my PhD, I worked as a researcher at the **Korea Institute for Health and Social Affairs** (2017–2018) and as a research assistant at the **Korea Labor Institute** (2016–2017).

## Methods and tools

Quantitative and computational social science; analysis of population and survey data; in-depth interviews. Programming and analysis in **Python, R, SQL, and Stata**.

## Teaching

Instructor of record for _Introduction to Sociology_, _Gender, Power, Inequality_, and _The Good Society_ at the University of Arizona. [Full teaching experience]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }})
