Research

Research by Minyoung An on gender, skilled migration, career trajectories, political identity, and social policy. Publications, working papers, and current projects.

Research agenda · Gender, institutions, inequality

How institutions shape consequential choices

I study how gender and institutional inequality shape migration, professional opportunity, and political identity. My research brings together population and survey data, computational methods, and interviews to connect individual decisions with the social contexts in which they are made.

How does gender shape the decision to migrate or return?

I examine how inequality in origin and destination countries shapes global talent flows, educational selectivity, and the return migration of highly trained workers.

How do discussions of gender inequality in social media and online forums relate to migration intentions? This developing project explores computational approaches to studying the connection between public discourse and migration decisions.
